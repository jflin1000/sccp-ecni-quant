# sccp-ecni-quant

Auto quantification of short-chain chlorinated paraffins (SCCPs) from
unit-resolution GC-ECNI-MS chromatograms.

Drop a folder of named files in. CSV extracted-ion chromatograms are
integrated on the fixed 24-ion C10–C13 list (Cl5–Cl10). The pattern is
reconstructed by non-negative least squares (NNLS) against the calibration
mixtures, and the total concentration is also calculated with the Reth Fa–D'
chlorine-content line. Vendor raw files are recognised and rejected; readers
for them are still TODO.

The numerical path is inspired by CPxplorer / CP-Crawler pattern reconstruction
(Bogdal deconvolution, NNLS over every standard, no fixed-sum constraint) and
by the Reth, Zencak and Oehme ECNI calibration. It does not reproduce those
programs.

## Install

```bash
pip install -e ".[dev]"
pytest
sccp-ecni demo
```

`python -m sccp_ecni demo` is the same entry point. Python 3.10 or newer,
NumPy, and SciPy are required.

## Pipeline

```text
named folder
    → integrate CSV (vendor formats TODO)
    → blank subtraction and replicate average
    → NNLS deconvolution + Reth Fa–D'
    → result tables
```

```bash
sccp-ecni run path/to/batch -o path/to/results
sccp-ecni demo -o sccp_ecni_demo_out
sccp-ecni ions
sccp-ecni make-examples -o examples/synthetic_batch
```

File names are the sample list. See [docs/naming.md](docs/naming.md).

```text
std__sccp55__cl-55.5__conc-10__rep-1.csv
sample__river01__factor-500__rep-1.csv
blank__solvent__rep-1.csv
```

Each CSV is one injection: `rt_min` plus the 24 ions (`C10Cl5` … `C13Cl10`,
or the quantifier m/z), and an optional `ISTD` column.

## Method

### Quantifier ions

The target list is fixed: C10, C11, C12 and C13, each with Cl5 through Cl10.
The recorded mass is the most abundant nominal m/z of the [M−Cl]− cluster
(35Cl = 0.7576, 37Cl = 0.2424).

| Chain | Cl5 | Cl6 | Cl7 | Cl8 | Cl9 | Cl10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C10 | 279 | 313 | 347 | 381 | 417 | 451 |
| C11 | 293 | 327 | 361 | 395 | 431 | 465 |
| C12 | 307 | 341 | 375 | 409 | 445 | 479 |
| C13 | 321 | 355 | 389 | 423 | 459 | 493 |

`D_i`, the chlorine mass percent of formula group `i`, uses standard atomic
weights (C 12.011, H 1.00784, Cl 35.453). `cl-` on a standard file is the
declared certificate value and is not substituted for `D_i`.

### Integration

For each ion, the baseline is the median of the first and last 5% of the
trace and the area above that baseline is the trapezoidal integral. This
expects a chromatogram with quiet ends and the SCCP hump in between. There is
no peak-picking model and no vendor raw decoder.

### Fa and D'

With an internal standard, `Fa_i = A_i / A_ISTD`. Without one, `Fa_i = A_i`
(external calibration). `Fa = Σ Fa_i`.

```text
D' = Σ (Fa_i × D_i) / Σ Fa_i
```

`D'` is the chlorine content calculated from the ECNI areas, as in Reth et al.
(2005). It is usually higher than the certificate chlorine content, because
ECNI favours the more chlorinated groups. Replicates are averaged after the
mean blank `Fa` is subtracted and negative residues are clipped to zero.

### NNLS deconvolution

Each standard column is that mixture's `Fa` divided by its `conc-` value
(levels of the same mixture are averaged). For a sample vector `Fa`,

```text
min ||P c − Fa||₂    with c ≥ 0
concentration_nnls = Σ c_k
```

`c_k` is the concentration, in `conc-` units, of calibration mixture `k`.
Coefficients are not forced to sum to 1, so every standard in the folder can
be used together. `R²` is the goodness of pattern fit. This is the right
number when the sample really is a non-negative mixture of those calibration
profiles.

### Reth calibration

For each standard,

```text
RF = Fa / conc
RF = a × D' + b
```

The sample concentration on that line is

```text
concentration_reth = Fa / (a × D' + b)
```

The line is only valid inside the `D'` window of the standards. A non-positive
predicted `RF` is reported as an error. `result_* = concentration_* × factor`.
When `conc-` is present on a sample or QC, recovery is
`100 × concentration / conc` and does not include `factor`.

NNLS and Reth answer different questions. NNLS is exact for a noise-free
mixture of the calibration profiles. Reth is exact for a profile whose `D'`
and response sit on the fitted line, which includes each calibration mixture
itself. A mixture of two mixtures is not required to land on that line, so the
two concentrations are both reported. In the synthetic batch, `mix` is exactly
8 by NNLS and 7.06 by Reth.

## Synthetic batch

`examples/synthetic_batch` is generated by `sccp-ecni make-examples`. Three
standards use the commercial labels 51.5%, 55.5% and 63.0% Cl, while their
area-weighted `D'` values are 58, 62 and 68. Response factors sit on
`RF = 20 × D' − 1020`. The 55.5% standard has two replicates scaled by 0.98
and 1.02; the average is the ideal pattern. The blank is baseline only.

| Injection | What it is | NNLS | Reth |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `sccp51`, `sccp55`, `sccp63` | standards at `conc-10` | 10 | 10 |
| `pure55` | the 55.5% pattern at concentration 4 | 4 | 4 |
| `mix` | 3 of `sccp51` plus 5 of `sccp63` | 8 | 7.06 |
| `sediment` | same solution as `pure55`, `factor-2` | solution 4, result 8 | solution 4, result 8 |
| `check63` | QC of the 63% pattern at concentration 6 | 6 (100% recovery) | 6 |

## Result files

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `injections.csv` | One row per file: identity and the 24 raw areas |
| `responses.csv` | Blank-subtracted, replicate-averaged `Fa` |
| `calibration.csv` | Declared Cl, `D'`, and `RF` of each standard |
| `calibration_fit.json` | `a`, `b`, and `R²` of `RF = a × D' + b` |
| `quantification.csv` | NNLS and Reth concentrations, factor, recovery, coefficients |

| `warnings.txt` | Present only when a fit, a recovery, or a `D'` window looks weak |

# sccp-ecni-quant（中文）

单位质量分辨 GC-ECNI-MS 短链氯化石蜡（SCCP）自动定量。

把按规则命名的文件放进一个文件夹。程序对固定的 24 个 C10–C13 离子（Cl5–Cl10）积分 CSV 提取离子色谱，用非负最小二乘（NNLS）对照校准混合物重建同系物分布，并用 Reth Fa–D' 氯含量标准曲线计算总浓度。仪器原始格式会被识别并拒绝，对应的读取器仍是 TODO。

算法思路来自 CPxplorer / CP-Crawler 的谱图重建（Bogdal 解卷积；对全部标准做 NNLS，不强制系数和为 1）以及 Reth、Zencak、Oehme 的 ECNI 校准。本程序不复现那些软件的输出。

```bash
pip install -e ".[dev]"
pytest
sccp-ecni demo
```

`python -m sccp_ecni demo` 是同一个入口。需要 Python 3.10 及以上、NumPy 和 SciPy。

```text
blank__solvent__rep-1.csv
```

每个 CSV 是一针进样：`rt_min`、24 个离子（`C10Cl5` … `C13Cl10`，或定量离子的 m/z），以及可选的 `ISTD` 列。

## 方法

### 定量离子

目标列表是固定的：C10、C11、C12、C13，氯原子数均为 5 到 10。记录的质量是 [M−Cl]− 簇中丰度最大的名义 m/z（35Cl = 0.7576，37Cl = 0.2424）。

| 碳链 | Cl5 | Cl6 | Cl7 | Cl8 | Cl9 | Cl10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C10 | 279 | 313 | 347 | 381 | 417 | 451 |
| C11 | 293 | 327 | 361 | 395 | 431 | 465 |
| C12 | 307 | 341 | 375 | 409 | 445 | 479 |
| C13 | 321 | 355 | 389 | 423 | 459 | 493 |

`D_i` 是同系物组 `i` 的氯质量分数，用标准原子量计算（C 12.011，H 1.00784，Cl 35.453）。标准文件名上的 `cl-` 是证书标称值，不会拿去代替 `D_i`。

### 积分

每个离子的基线取色谱两端各 5% 数据点的中位数，基线以上的面积用梯形积分。这适用于两端回到基线、中间是 SCCP 包峰的色谱。没有峰检测模型，也没有原始数据解码器。

### Fa 与 D'

有内标时 `Fa_i = A_i / A_ISTD`；没有内标时 `Fa_i = A_i`（外标）。`Fa = Σ Fa_i`。

```text
D' = Σ (Fa_i × D_i) / Σ Fa_i
```

`D'` 是由 ECNI 峰面积算出的氯含量，即 Reth 等（2005）使用的计算氯含量。由于 ECNI 对高氯同系物响应更强，`D'` 通常高于证书氯含量。各文件先减去空白 `Fa` 的均值，负值截为零，再对重复样取平均。

### NNLS 解卷积

每个标准列是该混合物的 `Fa` 除以文件名中的 `conc-`（同一混合物的多个浓度水平会先平均）。对样品向量 `Fa`：

```text
min ||P c − Fa||₂    c ≥ 0
concentration_nnls = Σ c_k
```

`c_k` 是第 `k` 个校准混合物的浓度，单位与 `conc-` 相同。系数不强制加和为 1，因此文件夹里的全部标准可以同时参与。`R²` 是谱图拟合优度。样品确实是这些校准谱的非负组合时，这就是应报告的浓度。

### Reth 校准

对每个标准：

```text
RF = Fa / conc
RF = a × D' + b
```

样品在该直线上的浓度为

```text
concentration_reth = Fa / (a × D' + b)
```

该直线只在标准品的 `D'` 区间内使用。预测的 `RF` 若不是正数，程序会报错。`result_* = concentration_* × factor`。样品或质控文件如果写了 `conc-`，回收率是 `100 × concentration / conc`，不乘 `factor`。

NNLS 和 Reth 回答的不是同一个问题。无噪声时，NNLS 能准确给出校准谱的混合浓度；Reth 能准确回读落在拟合直线上的响应，包括每个校准混合物自身。两种混合物再混合，并不必然仍落在这条直线上，所以两个浓度都会输出。合成批次里的 `mix`，NNLS 为 8，Reth 为 7.06。

## 合成示例

`examples/synthetic_batch` 由 `sccp-ecni make-examples` 生成。三个标准的商品标称氯含量是 51.5%、55.5% 和 63.0%，面积加权 `D'` 分别是 58、62 和 68。响应因子落在 `RF = 20 × D' − 1020` 上。55.5% 标准有两针，分别缩放 0.98 和 1.02，平均后是理想谱。空白只有基线。

| 进样 | 含义 | NNLS | Reth |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `sccp51`、`sccp55`、`sccp63` | 浓度为 `conc-10` 的标准 | 10 | 10 |
| `pure55` | 55.5% 谱，浓度 4 | 4 | 4 |
| `mix` | 3 份 `sccp51` 加 5 份 `sccp63` | 8 | 7.06 |
| `sediment` | 与 `pure55` 相同的溶液，`factor-2` | 溶液 4，结果 8 | 溶液 4，结果 8 |
| `check63` | 63% 谱的质控，浓度 6 | 6（回收率 100%） | 6 |
